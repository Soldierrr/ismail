SME Tax Compliance Panel, 2015–2024

TaxationPanel dataNigeria

This panel dataset tracks tax filing behaviour, compliance costs and audit exposure for 4,200 registered small and medium enterprises between 2015 and 2024. It was compiled from anonymised tax authority records and a supplementary annual survey of firm owners.

Variables include annual turnover, filing frequency, penalty history, sector classification and self-reported compliance burden. The dataset is fully anonymised and cleared for academic use.
