About the researcher

Twenty years of fieldwork, now open for reuse

Dr. Kenton has spent two decades researching tax compliance, audit practice and corporate governance across emerging markets. Ledger was built to make that fieldwork — and the datasets built by colleagues working in the same space — available directly to the researchers who need it, without the delay of formal publication.

Every dataset on Ledger has been cleaned, documented and anonymised to the same standard used in Dr. Kenton's own published work, so you can build on it with confidence.

Credentials

  • PhD in Accounting, University of Lagos
  • Associate Professor, Department of Accounting
  • 15+ peer-reviewed publications in taxation and governance research
  • Consultant to national tax authorities on SME compliance
